Bio, Resume & Artistic Statement

Biography

Anni Chen is a contemporary visual artist and designer based in Boston, Massachusetts. Growing up surrounded by a blend of classical aesthetics and modern architecture, Anni developed a deep fascination with how environments shape human emotion and storytelling.

She pursued formal training in fine arts and digital media design, refining her expertise across brand identity, illustration, and fine art painting. Her technical background empowers her to bridge traditional mediums with modern digital workflows seamlessly.

Today, Anni's creative practice spans commissioned brand identities, conceptual digital illustrations, and expressive canvas paintings. Her studio work explores the dynamic tension between structured geometric systems and fluid organic expressions.

Anni's artwork and design pieces have been featured in various regional exhibitions, digital publications, and private collections, continuously striving to create meaningful visual experiences that resonate deeply with audiences.

Artist's Statement

"Art is the meeting point of analytical structure and emotional release. Through my work, I aim to document the invisible currents of modern life—translating urban rhythms, light, and technology into visual harmonies that invite viewers to pause, reflect, and reconnect."
