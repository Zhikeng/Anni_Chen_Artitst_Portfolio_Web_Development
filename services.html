Operational Support & Hours

To ensure smooth studio operations, community collaboration, and project management, various support categories and schedules are maintained below:

  1. Transportation & Logistics
    • Art Delivery Van Pickup: Mondays & Thursdays, 9:00 AM - 12:00 PM
    • Exhibition Cargo Dispatch: Wednesdays, 1:00 PM - 4:00 PM
    • Local Courier Drop-offs: Daily, 2:00 PM - 5:00 PM
  2. Studio Maintenance & Facilities
    • General Deep Cleaning & Sanitation: Tuesdays, 6:00 AM - 9:00 AM
    • Equipment Calibration & Hardware Checks: Fridays, 4:00 PM - 6:00 PM
    • HVAC and Lighting Inspection: Bi-weekly on the 1st and 3rd Monday, 8:00 AM - 10:00 AM
  3. Cleaning & Waste Management
    • Recycling & Material Sorting: Daily, 5:00 PM - 6:00 PM
    • Hazardous Material / Chemical Disposal: Every Friday, 5:00 PM - 7:00 PM
    • Floor Care & Surface Polishing: Saturdays, 8:00 AM - 11:00 AM
  4. Workshops, Lessons & Community Events
    • Digital Art Masterclass Lessons: Saturdays, 10:00 AM - 1:00 PM
    • Open Studio Critique Sessions: Thursdays, 3:00 PM - 5:00 PM
    • Quarterly Creative Competitions: First Saturday of every season, 12:00 PM - 6:00 PM
